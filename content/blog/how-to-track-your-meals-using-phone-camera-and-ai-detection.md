# Track Your Meals in Under a Minute with AI-Powered Camera Detection

Nutrition and fitness app abandonment rates are alarmingly high—**71% of users quit within three months** of downloading their app of choice, with **46% abandoning it entirely after just 24 hours**[8][11]. The primary culprit? **Time-consuming manual entry** and overly complex tracking processes[9][24]. Get Movin AI solves this problem by letting you log meals in under 60 seconds using your phone’s camera and advanced AI detection.

## Why Traditional Nutrition Tracking Fails

Manual food logging demands meticulous portion measurement and database search. Studies show:

- **High burden leads to dropout**: Nearly three-quarters of users stop using nutrition or fitness apps within the first three months due to the time required for manual entry[8][11].
- **Complex interfaces overwhelm**: Users cite “frustrating navigation” and “information overload” as key abandonment drivers[24].
- **Simplified tracking boosts adherence**: A pilot trial found that a **simplified, photo-based monitoring approach yielded comparable weight loss** (4.0% vs. 5.7%) and adherence to standard calorie counting over six months[7].

Get Movin AI’s camera-meal feature eliminates manual entry entirely, reducing friction and keeping you engaged.

## How to Log a Meal with Your Phone Camera

1. **Open the Get Movin AI app** and tap the **Energy** section  
   ![GetMovinAI energy page](/images/tutorials/camera-meal/1.png)
2. Scroll down to **Meals**, then tap **Log Meal**  
   ![GetMovinAI meals section](/images/tutorials/camera-meal/2.png)
3. Choose **Scan with Camera**  
   ![GetMovinAI meal logging options](/images/tutorials/camera-meal/3.png)
4. Allow camera permissions and wait for the camera to appear
   ![GetMovinAI scan with camera](/images/tutorials/camera-meal/4.png)
5. Snap a photo of your meal, then tap **Confirm & Analyze**  
   ![GetMovinAI scanned meal with camera](/images/tutorials/camera-meal/5.png)
6. The AI processes the image—just wait a few seconds  
   ![GetMovinAI meal analyzing](/images/tutorials/camera-meal/6.png)
7. **BOOM!** Your meal’s ingredients, energy, macros, and nutrition score are displayed  
   ![GetMovinAI meal nutrition](/images/tutorials/camera-meal/7.png)

Everything takes **less than one minute**, so logging stays simple and effortless.

## Why Get Movin AI Solves a Big Problem

By automating meal logging, Get Movin AI addresses the top reasons for nutrition-tracking abandonment:

- **Speed & simplicity**: Logging takes under 60 seconds versus 15–23 minutes of manual entry[13].
- **Accuracy & personalization**: AI-driven detection identifies ingredients and portion sizes without guesswork[9].
- **Engagement & retention**: Reduced friction keeps users logging consistently, combating the typical three-month drop-off[8][11].

Get Movin AI transforms a tedious chore into a seamless habit—so you can focus on your goals, not on the app.
